Issued certificate policy

Used by pull-mode's GetCertificateStatus to tell an application it's time to renew.

How far out the Certificate Revocation List's own nextUpdate is set each time it's (re)generated -- this GDS always builds it fresh from the current revoked list, so there's no separate refresh interval to configure.

Push-mode auto re-provisioning

Push-mode devices never call the GDS back to ask "am I still good?" the way pull-mode applications do with GetCertificateStatus -- so without this, a device that lost its certificate entirely (a factory reset, a config wipe) would stay uncertified until someone noticed and pushed manually. This checks every configured push client in the background and re-pushes automatically if its certificate isn't ours anymore, is expired, or is due for renewal.
